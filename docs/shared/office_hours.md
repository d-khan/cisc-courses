### Technical Assistance

If you are joining office hours for technical assistance:

- Use a **desktop or laptop computer** rather than a phone or tablet.
- Make sure you have a **reliable internet connection**.
- Make sure **screen sharing is enabled and working in Zoom** before joining.
- If you have not used Zoom screen sharing before, your operating system may require you to grant Zoom permission to share your screen. Please configure and test these permissions **before joining office hours**.
- Have your **code, assignment, error messages, or other relevant files** open and ready to share.

Being prepared to share your screen will make troubleshooting programming and technical issues much easier.

## Office Hours Preparation Checklist

Before joining office hours, make sure you:

- [ ] Know what question or topic you need help with
- [ ] Have attempted the problem on your own
- [ ] Reviewed the relevant lecture notes or examples
- [ ] Have your assignment, lab, or quiz open
- [ ] Have your code ready to show, if applicable
- [ ] Have noted any error messages or confusing steps
- [ ] Can explain what you expected to happen and what actually happened
- [ ] Have something available to take notes

> 💡 **Tip:** Office hours are intended to help you understand concepts and work through problems. Come prepared with specific questions so we can use the time effectively.
